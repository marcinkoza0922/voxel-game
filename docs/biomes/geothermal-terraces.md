# Geothermal terraces

Proposed direction.

## Concept

Pale mineral terraces hold turquoise pools, with steam rising from the upper
springs. Overflow spills down the steps and gives players a route to follow
uphill from the lowest pools to the spring.

## Open design questions

- How wide should the terraces be, and how many pools should share a spring?
- Should hot water affect traversal or offer a reason to stop at certain pools?
