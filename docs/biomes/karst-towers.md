# Karst towers

Proposed direction.

## Concept

Tall limestone pillars rise from a flat, green river valley. Natural arches
and caves cut through some towers. A river could disappear into a tower and
connect to [limestone karst](limestone-karst.md) underground.

## Open design questions

- How close should towers stand, and where should gaps leave room to follow the river?
- How often should caves and arches form routes through a tower?
