# Flooded grasslands

Proposed direction.

## Concept

An open grass plain broken by shallow water, branching channels, and scattered
dry islands. Raised grassy strips give players routes to cross on foot.
Grazing herds could follow the same strips between patches of dry pasture.

## Open design questions

- How much of the plain should stay dry, and how often should channels interrupt foot routes?
- Should flooding be permanent or vary over time?
