# Rainbow mountains

Suggestion for consideration.

## Concept

Dry ridges and slopes expose broad bands of colored stone that bend and tilt
across the landscape. Try ochre, rust red, cream, and muted green. A few wide
bands should be visible across a valley, with individual layers also visible
in ravine walls. The Aladaghlar Mountains of northwestern Iran are the visual
reference. Tectonic folding shaped the rock layers, and erosion exposed them.
[NASA Earth Observatory](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/folded-rocks-of-northwest-iran-152347/)

## Open design questions

- Should these form their own dry mountain regions or appear as rare ranges
  within the [Badlands](badlands.md)?
- Which colors and band widths read well at voxel scale and from a distance?
- Should players be able to collect the colored stone for building?
