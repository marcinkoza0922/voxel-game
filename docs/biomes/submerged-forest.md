# Submerged forest

Proposed direction.

## Concept

Bare trunks and branches rise from the floor of a drowned valley. Divers can
weave through the old canopy or descend to follow the former river channel
along the bottom.

## Open design questions

- Should some crowns break the surface, and what depth range makes the valley readable?
- How dense should submerged branches be along common swimming routes?
