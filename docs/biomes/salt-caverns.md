# Salt caverns

Proposed direction.

## Concept

Broad chambers have pale walls, translucent salt seams, and dark brine pools.
Some caverns could lie beneath [salt flats](salt-flats.md), with entrances
along the basin rim.

## Open design questions

- How often should salt caverns connect to surface salt flats?
- Should players be able to mine salt seams, and should brine affect traversal?
