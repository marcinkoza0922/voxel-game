# Polar

Polar is the permanently snow-covered climate. It spans plains, hills, mountains,
and mega mountains in a world without geographic poles.

## Terrain and wildlife

- **Glaciers** follow some polar mountain slopes. Broad tongues of blue ice
  taper downhill and split into narrow crevasses. Some open into ice caverns.
- **Penguins** gather in small colonies near polar shores and frozen ponds.
  They waddle across the banks and scatter when approached.
- **Polar bears** roam alone along shores and across open snowfields. They
  usually leave the player alone and retaliate when attacked. They spawn away
  from penguin colonies.

Swimming and belly-sliding are future possibilities. See the
